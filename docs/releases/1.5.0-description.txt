A full month calendar watchface with tons of customizability. The time up top, a full month calendar below, and a status line with customizable info. Inspired by the old Calendar Watchface by Willian Heaton, updated and extended for the 2026 Pebble watches.

• Full month grid with today highlighted; optionally fill in the neighboring months
• Banner shows the month or full date
• Your next calendar event with a countdown, in the banner, on its own line, or in the status line
• 12-hour time (without leading zero) or 24-hour time
• Different font options in three sizes; optional seconds
• Whichever status items you want: battery, phone battery, weather, steps, distance, heart rate, active minutes, calories, sleep, week number, next alarm, disconnected alert, and readings from your other Pebble apps
• Weather from your Pebble app's weather provider, with optional high/low or hourly readings
• Calendar event markers from up to three iCal/ICS links, color-coded per calendar
• Different color themes or custom colors, high contrast, readable with the backlight off
• Visual settings page with a live preview of your watchface
• Compresses under Timeline Quick View instead of getting cropped
• Round watches also supported, layout adapts as needed

Next event, phone battery, and readings from other apps need Pebble app 2.0 or later. Calendar, location, and health access are each asked for only when first needed, and the watchface works without them.

Tested on my Pebble Time 2, but should work on any pebble, old or new.  If you run it on another model and hit an issue, let me know!
