MonthGrid 1.4.0

• New visual settings with a live preview of your watchface.
• Choose where your next event appears and how early it shows.
• Separate watch and phone battery readings.
• Weather range and hourly options when the Pebble app supplies them.
• Improved Pixel font sizing, calendar previews, and round-screen layout.
• More consistent handling of all-day events and daylight-saving time.

Next events, phone battery, and readings from other apps require compatible Pebble app support. Calendar access depends on permissions; the plugin-enabled download is available separately on GitHub.
